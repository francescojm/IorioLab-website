Join us

Come build predictive perturbational biology with us.

We are interested in people who want to work across disciplinary boundaries — from statistical learning and computational genomics to CRISPR screening and experimental cancer biology.

What we look for

Scientific curiosity, quantitative thinking, independence and a willingness to engage deeply with both the computational and biological sides of a problem.

Opportunities

The production site can surface open positions here, alongside guidance for postdoctoral, PhD, MSc and visiting applications. Current vacancies should be linked to the official Human Technopole posting.

Environment

The lab is based in the Computational Biology Research Centre at Human Technopole in Milan and works closely with experimental and computational collaborators across the institute and internationally.
