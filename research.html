Research

Learning the rules of cancer vulnerability.

Our research combines perturbational experiments and computational models to move from descriptive maps of dependencies toward mechanistic and predictive models of cellular response.

DepSHOCK

Acute loss of oncogenic dependencies

Longitudinal transcriptional profiling following tightly controlled gene knockout, with the goal of separating editing-associated, generic death and dependency-specific response programmes.

CRISPR-VUS / DAM

Functional interpretation of cancer variation

Large-scale functional evidence linking variants of uncertain significance to genetic dependencies, molecular contexts and therapeutic hypotheses.

Genetic interactions

Prediction beyond measured gene pairs

Methods that combine genome-wide single-gene perturbations with limited combinatorial screens to infer genetic interactions with calibrated uncertainty.

Drug sensitivity & recovery

Vulnerabilities created by adaptation

Functional and molecular profiling of drug-resistant and recovering cancer models to identify acquired and reversible dependencies.
